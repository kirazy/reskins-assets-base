Version: 2.2.0
Date: ????
  Features:
    - Added all Factorio 2.0 centrifuge sprites to `graphics/entity/centrifuge`; the entity has been re-rendered in Factorio 2.1.
    - Added all Factorio 2.0 heat exchanger sprites to `graphics/entity/heat-exchanger` to support use of the old model in Factorio 2.1.
    - Added all Factorio 2.0 oil refinery sprites to `graphics/entity/oil-refinery` to support use of the old model in Factorio 2.1.
    - Added new tintable sprite sets for the pumpjack, allowing to procedurally tint the entire structure, the standard stripe, or the accented portions of the structure.
      - Includes the Factorio 2.1 pumpjack sprites in `graphics/entity/pumpjack` to support use of the new model in Factorio 2.0.
    - Added new tintable sprites for the heat pipe, allowing to procedurally tint the heat pipes and the metal plates separately.
    - Added new tintable sprites for the lab and related technology research speed.
    - Added tintable boiler fire sprites and icon layers.
  Breaking Changes:
    - Removed `graphics/entity/pumpjack/pumpjack-horsehead-mask.png`.
    - Removed `graphics/entity/pumpjack/pumpjack-horsehead-highlights.png`.
    - Removed `-icon` and `-technology` discriminators from icon and technology file names; for example, `pipe-to-ground-icon.png` is now simply `pipe-to-ground.png`.
    - Numerous files have been renamed to conform to the naming conventions defined by Artisanal Reskins: Assets API.
