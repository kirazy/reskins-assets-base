Version: 2.3.0
Date: 2026-11-09
  Features:
    - Added updated tintable sprites for the centrifuge.
    - Added frozen sprites for the electric furnace, the 2, 4, and 6 slot beacons, and the transport belts.
    - Added the turbo transport belt sprites.
    - Added the battery, lab, radar, and repair pack technology icons.

    - Updated the electric furnace remnants sprites using the revised mask process.
  Breaking Changes:
    - Renamed the base layer of the oil gathering technology icon to `graphics/technology/oil-gathering/oil-gathering-base-base.png`.
    - Renamed the technology icons of the quality module to `graphics/technology/modules/5-lights/quality/quality-{tier}.png`.

Version: 2.2.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Features:
    - Added new tintable sprite sets for the pumpjack, allowing to procedurally tint the entire structure, the standard stripe, or the accented portions of the structure.
    - Added new tintable sprites for the heat pipe, allowing to procedurally tint the heat pipes and the metal plates separately.
    - Added new tintable sprites for the lab and related technology research speed.
    - Added tintable boiler fire sprites and icon layers.
    - Added tintable sprites for assembling machines 1, 2 and 3, including tintable status lights.
  Breaking Changes:
    - Removed `graphics/entity/pumpjack/pumpjack-horsehead-mask.png`.
    - Removed `graphics/entity/pumpjack/pumpjack-horsehead-highlights.png`.
    - Removed `-icon` and `-technology` discriminators from icon and technology file names; for example, `pipe-to-ground-icon.png` is now simply `pipe-to-ground.png`.
    - Numerous files have been renamed to conform to the naming conventions defined by Artisanal Reskins: Assets API.
    - The assembling machine, heat exchanger, and oil refinery sprites for classic models have been moved to Artisanal Reskins: Assorted Assets.
